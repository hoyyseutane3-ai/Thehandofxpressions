Common Inquiries

Bantse's Q&A

What portrait sizes are available?

Commissions can be booked for A4, A3, A2, and large A1 canvas options. Each piece is executed on professional grade paper with custom, high-contrast graphite realism.

How do delivery and collection work?

We offer local order pickup if you are located in Pretoria. For portrait commissions requested across the rest of South Africa, your completed artwork is securely packaged and dispatched via PostNet courier (+R100).

What payment methods do you accept?

Secure electronic funds transfers (EFT) can be processed straight to our Capitec Bank studio account. Full details including the account number are displayed at the bottom of the page.
