Privacy Policy & Cookie Disclosures

When you place a custom artwork commission, submit contact forms, or communicate via WhatsApp/Email, we collect limited personal details including your name, contact phone number, email address, delivery location, and reference photos provided for artwork creation.

3. POPIA Compliance (South Africa)

In accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa, any customer photos or personal contact details provided to our studio are used exclusively for fulfilling artwork orders, issuing quotes, and processing PostNet delivery services. We never sell, rent, or distribute your private information to third parties.

4. Data Retention & Image Rights

Reference photographs supplied for drawings are kept on secure studio systems during the creation period. Finished artwork photos may be shared on our official studio showcase pages (e.g., TikTok, Gallery) unless specifically requested otherwise in writing prior to order completion.

Due to the personalized nature of custom hand-drawn art, orders cannot be canceled or fully refunded once drawing work has commenced. If an item arrives damaged due to transport handling, clients must notify us within 24 hours with photos for studio inspection and remediation.

About Bantse's Pencil Studio

TheHandOfXpressions • Pretoria, South Africa

Bantse's Pencil (operating under TheHandOfXpressions) is an independent South African fine art studio located in Pretoria. Founded and managed by lead artist Bantse Mampana, the studio specializes in precision realism portraiture using technical graphite and deep charcoal mediums.
